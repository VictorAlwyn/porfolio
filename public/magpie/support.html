Magpie help and support

Last updated 22 October 2026

Need a hand? Email alwyn.tablatin@gmail.com. Please say which phone you have and what happened. Do not send your notes or recordings; they stay on your phone and I do not need them.

The first start downloads AI models

Magpie works on your phone, so on first start you tap Download to fetch the AI models it needs. Use Wi-Fi and make sure the phone has this much free space, plus some room to spare:

ChoiceDownloadFor
Standardabout 3.5 GBThe default on every phone.
Built-inabout 0.4 GBiPhones on iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on. Uses Apple's on-device model for summaries and Ask Magpie.

If a download stops, tap Try again (or Download if you closed Magpie). Models that already finished are kept. On Android the unfinished file usually carries on where it stopped; on iPhone it starts again. Under Privacy → AI models you can delete models you no longer use, and on iPhone change your choice.

Working without internet

Once the models are downloaded, recording, transcribing, summaries, search and Ask Magpie all work in airplane mode.

My note is still “Transcribing” or “Writing the summary”

Everything is done by the phone itself, so longer recordings can take a few minutes and use battery. Notes are processed one at a time. If you close Magpie before they finish, they carry on the next time you open it. With Built-in, summaries wait while Apple Intelligence is turned off or not ready.

Answers can be wrong

Ask Magpie answers from your notes using a small model on your phone. It can miss things or misread them, so check the notes it lists under each answer. If an answer is wrong, tap Report this answer. It opens an email draft with the question and the answer; nothing is sent until you send it.

Moving to a new phone

Your notes are not in iCloud or Android backups, and Magpie has no account, so they only move if you move them:

  • On the old phone: Privacy → Export and backup → Save encrypted backup. Choose a password of at least 8 characters.
  • Copy the file to the new phone.
  • On the new phone: install Magpie, finish setup, open Privacy → Export and backup, type the backup password under Restore and tap Choose a backup to restore.

Magpie never stores your backup password, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered. A restore adds the notes in the backup and leaves the notes already on the phone as they are.

Getting your notes out

Under Export and backup you can save all notes as Markdown or JSON files. Those files are not encrypted.

App lock

Turn it on under Privacy. Magpie asks for Face ID, a fingerprint or your phone's passcode when you come back to it after the delay you choose.

Deleting everything

Privacy → Delete everything on this phone erases the notes, recordings, search data, database key and settings, and can remove the downloaded models too. It cannot be undone.

Refunds

Magpie is a paid download. On iPhone, Apple handles refunds: go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, ask from your Google Play order history: play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory. If Google Play sends you to the developer, email me.
